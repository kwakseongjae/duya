## 1. Competitor packaging teardown (2026)

### KO summary
2026 콜드아웃리치 SaaS는 **헤드라인 가격 ≠ 실제 청구액**이 관례다. Instantly는 Outreach·Credits·CRM 모듈 분리, Smartlead는 워크스페이스/검증 애드온, Lemlist는 per-user + 크레딕, Apollo는 per-seat + 크레딕 미터, Hunter는 크레딕 풀 + 연결된 발송 계정 캡. 에이전시 함정은 **화이트라벨·클라이언트 워크스페이스·별도 리드 크레딕**에서 난다.

### 1.1 Instantly.ai — modular stack, unlimited inboxes bait

**Official:** [https://instantly.ai/pricing](https://instantly.ai/pricing)  
**Secondary:** [Woodpecker Instantly pricing 2026](https://woodpecker.co/blog/instantly-ai-pricing/), [Landbase Instantly pricing 2026](https://www.landbase.com/blog/instantly-ai-pricing), [Cleanlist Instantly guide (2026-09-01)](https://www.cleanlist.ai/blog/2026-09-01-instantly-pricing-guide), [Cold Email Manifesto](https://coldemailmanifesto.com/instantly-ai-pricing)

#### Outreach (sending) — monthly / annual equiv.

| Tier | Monthly | Annual ≈/mo | Uploaded contacts | Emails / mo | Headline inclusions |
|------|---------|-------------|-------------------|-------------|---------------------|
| Growth | $47 | $37.60 | 1,000 | 5,000 | Unlimited email accounts + warmup, chat support |
| Hypergrowth | $97 | $77.60 | 25,000 | 100k–125k* | A/B, Unibox (higher tiers), premium support |
| Light Speed | $358 | $286.30 | 100,000+ | 500,000+ | **SISR** (Server & IP Sharding/Rotation), private IP pools |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | 100k+ | 500k+ | Dedicated AM, Slack, private deliverability network |

\*Hypergrowth email cap listed as **100,000** on annual compare tables and **125,000** on some monthly renders on Instantly’s page — confirm at checkout ([Cleanlist](https://www.cleanlist.ai/blog/2026-09-01-instantly-pricing-guide)).

**All Outreach plans market:** unlimited connected accounts + unlimited warmup — pricing scales by **volume (emails/contacts)**, not seats.

#### Instantly Credits (lead DB / AI agents — formerly SuperSearch)

| Tier | Monthly | Annual ≈/mo | Credits / mo (ranges cited) |
|------|---------|-------------|-----------------------------|
| Growth Credits | $47 | $42.30 (or $37.60 in some renders) | ~1,500–2,000 |
| Supersonic | $97 | $87.30 | ~5,000–7,500 |
| Hyper Credits | from $197 | $177.30 | 10k–200k |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | 200k+ |

Includes 450M+ B2B DB, waterfall enrichment, Web Researcher / Email Writer agents, LLM access ([instantly.ai/pricing](https://instantly.ai/pricing)).

#### CRM (separate module)

| Tier | Monthly | Annual ≈/mo | Notes |
|------|---------|-------------|-------|
| Growth CRM | $47 | $37.90 | Unlimited seats, Unibox, reporting |
| Hyper CRM | $97 | $77.60 | Calling, SMS, visitors, Salesflows |

#### Bundles on pricing page (Outreach + Credits packaged)

| Bundle | Monthly | Rough contents (from page) |
|--------|---------|----------------------------|
| Starter | $94 ($85 annual) | Growth-class send + ~1,500 credits |
| Scale (most popular) | $194 ($175 annual) | Hypergrowth-class send + ~5,000 credits + AI Reply Agent |
| Agency | $555 ($500 annual) | Light Speed-class send + ~10k credits + SISR |

#### Credit traps & agency surprises

1. **Three meters:** Outreach + Credits + CRM stack independently. Entry “$47” becomes ~$94–$150+ once leads (and optionally CRM) are added ([Woodpecker](https://woodpecker.co/blog/instantly-ai-pricing/), [Cold Email Manifesto](https://coldemailmanifesto.com/instantly-ai-pricing)).
2. **Growth contact/email caps are tiny** (1k contacts / 5k emails) — serious outbound jumps to Hypergrowth immediately.
3. **AI agents burn Credits** — Reply Agent / research is not “included forever” in Outreach alone ([Instantly AI Reply Agent blog](https://instantly.ai/blog/ai-reply-agent-pricing-for-agencies/)).
4. **SISR only on Light Speed / Agency** — private IP story is a $358+ upsell.
5. **No permanent free tier** — “no credit card” = trial, not forever-free ([Cleanlist](https://www.cleanlist.ai/blog/2026-09-01-instantly-pricing-guide)).
6. **Agency surprise:** client isolation / white-label and lead volume still require Credits + higher Outreach; mailbox infra (domains/Workspace) is *outside* Instantly’s sticker price.

**Implication for us:** Do **not** compete on unlimited inboxes. Compete on clarity of one meter (Issues / sends) and HITL Memory/MCP that Instantly prices as separate AI credits.

**Implication for us:** Agency SKU must include **N client workspaces in base price** (or honest per-workspace add-on with a free included allotment). Never hide workspace tax.

### 1.3 Lemlist — per-user + pay-per-success credits

**Official:** [https://www.lemlist.com/pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing)  
**Credits deep dive:** [lemlist blog — leads cost 2026](https://www.lemlist.com/blog/how-much-do-leads-cost-in-lemlist)  
**Secondary:** [RevenueFlow lemlist pricing](https://www.revenueflow.com/blog/lemlist-pricing), [Costbench](https://costbench.com/software/sales-engagement/lemlist/)

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly ≈/mo | Basis | Sending |
|------|---------|-------------|-------|---------|
| Email | $69 | $55 | Unlimited users & email senders | **50,000 emails/mo** (higher volumes: $89 / $159 / $359 / $659 per lemlist blog table) |
| Multichannel (popular) | $109 | $87 | **Per user**, 5 senders/user | Unlimited emails & messages |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | ≥5 senders/user, SSO/SAML, DAM | Unlimited |

**14-day Multichannel trial**, no card; after trial cannot send new campaigns but keep account data ([lemlist pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing)).

#### Credits (1 credit = $0.01; charged on success)

| Action | Credits | ≈ USD |
|--------|---------|-------|
| Email finder & verifier | 5 | $0.05 |
| Email verification only | 1 | $0.01 |
| LinkedIn enrichment | 1 | $0.01 |
| Phone finder | 20 | $0.20 |
| AI variables | 1 | $0.01 |
| SMS | 6 | $0.06 |
| Calling | 4–32 / billed minute | varies |
| Website visitor signal | 20 / company | $0.20 |

**Included free credits:** Trial 200 one-time; **Email plan: none**; Multichannel **400 credits/user/mo**; Enterprise 400+ (new) ([lemlist credits guide](https://www.lemlist.com/blog/how-much-do-leads-cost-in-lemlist)). Free credits do **not** roll over; purchased credits do.

#### Traps

1. **Email plan looks “unlimited users” but zero included enrichment credits** — data is pure PAYG.
2. **Multichannel flips to per-seat** — cost multiplies with team size.
3. **Signals / AI / phone** silently drain credits; intent signals can be 100–400 credits each ([pricing page + blog](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing)).
4. **WhatsApp is an add-on** on Multichannel.
5. MCP / API listed on Email+ plans — good parity signal that **agent access is becoming a packaging feature**, not a free giveaway forever.

**Implication for us:** Prefer **workspace flat fee + clear send/Issue caps**, optional BYO LLM key (like lemlist’s OpenAI bypass). Gate MCP on Pro+, not Free.

### 1.4 Apollo.io — per-seat + multi credit meters + fair-use “unlimited”

**Official:** [https://www.apollo.io/pricing](https://www.apollo.io/pricing) (page is heavy SPA; numbers below from 2026 teardowns that cite the page)  
**Secondary:** [Salesmotion Apollo 2026](https://salesmotion.io/blog/apollo-pricing), [Salesgear Apollo 2026](https://www.salesgear.io/blog/apollo-pricing/), [JoinValley credits explained](https://www.joinvalley.co/blog/apollo-pricing-and-credits-explained-2026), [SyncGTM review](https://syncgtm.com/blog/apollo-io-review)

| Plan | Annual $/user/mo | Monthly $/user/mo (approx) | Notes |
|------|------------------|----------------------------|-------|
| Free / Starter | $0 | $0 | Sequences limited; Gmail-only on non-paying; export/mobile scarce |
| Basic | $49 | ~$59 | CRM integrations; unlimited email credits under **fair use** |
| Professional | $79 | ~$99 | US dialer, advanced reports, AI writing |
| Organization | $119 | ~$149 | **Min 3 seats**; international dialer, custom reports, SSO |

#### Credit traps (critical)

1. **Per-seat multiplication** — 5× Professional ≈ $395–$495/mo seats before add-ons ([JoinValley](https://www.joinvalley.co/blog/apollo-pricing-and-credits-explained-2026)).
2. **Multiple meters:** email / mobile / export (legacy) or unified pool (newer accounts) — same plan, different experience depending on account vintage ([Salesgear](https://www.salesgear.io/blog/apollo-pricing/)).
3. **No rollover** on monthly allowances ([JoinValley](https://www.joinvalley.co/blog/apollo-pricing-and-credits-explained-2026)).
4. **Export credits** — taking data out of Apollo (CSV/CRM) burns a scarce meter; working *inside* Apollo feels cheap.
5. **“Unlimited” fair use:** non-paying Unlimited-style caps ~10k credits/account/mo; paying Unlimited ≈ lesser of `$paid / $0.025` or 1M credits/year ([Apollo FAQ on pricing page](https://www.apollo.io/pricing)).
6. **Organization 3-seat minimum** → real floor ~$357/mo annual billing ([Salesgear](https://www.salesgear.io/blog/apollo-pricing/)).
7. **External product / agency resale** of Apollo data requires custom agreement ([Apollo pricing FAQ](https://www.apollo.io/pricing)).

**Implication for us:** Avoid per-seat for MVP ICP (indie + small agency). Prefer workspace pricing. Be explicit that **BYO data** (CSV / Hunter / Clay) is first-class so we never trap users in an export meter.

### 1.5 Hunter.io — simple credit pool, hard sending-account ceiling

**Official:** [https://hunter.io/pricing](https://hunter.io/pricing)  
**Help:** [All-in-one outreach plans](https://help.hunter.io/en/articles/6001212-hunter-all-in-one-outreach-plans)  
**Secondary:** [Emailchaser Hunter 2026](https://www.emailchaser.com/learn/hunter-io-pricing), [GrowthHackSuite](https://growthhacksuite.com/hunter-io-pricing-model)

#### All-in-one Outreach plans

| Plan | Monthly | Annual ≈/mo | Credits / mo | Connected email accounts | Recipients / sequence |
|------|---------|-------------|--------------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 50 | 1 | 500 |
| Starter | $49 | $34 (−30%) | 2,000 | 3 | 2,500 |
| Growth | $149 | $104 | 10,000 | 10 | 5,000 |
| Scale | $299 | $209 | 25,000 | 20 | 15,000 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom | Custom | Custom |

**Credit rules (official FAQ):** 1 credit = 1 email found; 0.5 credit = 1 verification; Bulk Domain Search 1 credit ≤10 emails; same search counted once per billing period ([hunter.io/pricing](https://hunter.io/pricing)). **Unlimited team members** share one credit pool. Extra email account slots from **~$10/mo**. MCP listed on feature matrix.

**Data Platform plans:** API-only Search/Verification credits; **no** Sequences / Discover / Signals ([Hunter help](https://help.hunter.io/en/articles/9920233-which-plan-is-right-for-me-all-in-one-outreach-platform-plan-or-a-data-platform-plan)).

#### Traps

1. **Connected accounts capped at 20** even on Scale $299 — blockers for high-rotation cold ops ([Emailchaser](https://www.emailchaser.com/learn/hunter-io-pricing)).
2. Monthly credits **reset**; annual buys yearly pool (no monthly reset) — billing mode changes burn rate.
3. Sequences are basic vs Instantly/Smartlead — Hunter wins on finder accuracy + free tier, loses on infra scale.
4. Inbox Protection / managed accounts are **add-ons**.

**Implication for us:** Free 50-credit Hunter wedge is the gold standard for **data tools**. For a *sending* Issue-board product, Free should prove Board + Memory with low send caps — not try to match Hunter’s finder.

## 3. Free tier · waitlist · open-source wedges

### KO summary
성공 패턴: (1) **진짜 Free**로 신뢰(Hunter/Apollo/Resend), (2) **Waitlist + payment intent**로 검증, (3) **오픈소스/템플릿 웨지**로 개발자·에이전트 채널(Postiz, Cal.com, Ghost). 우리 제품은 전체 시퀀서 OS화보다 **Issue board + MCP 스키마 + AGENTS.md** 부분 오픈이 맞다.

### 3.1 Free tiers that worked (patterns, not promises)

| Product | Free wedge | Why it converts | Source |
|---------|------------|-----------------|--------|
| **Hunter** | 50 credits/mo forever | Real finder utility; unlimited teammates sharing pool | [hunter.io/pricing](https://hunter.io/pricing) |
| **Apollo** | Free forever + sequences (limited) | Database try-before-buy; Gmail-only friction to paid | [apollo.io/pricing](https://www.apollo.io/pricing), [Salesgear](https://www.salesgear.io/blog/apollo-pricing/) |
| **Resend** | 3,000 emails/mo · **100/day** | Developer habit; daily cap teaches discipline | [resend.com/pricing](https://www.resend.com/pricing) |
| **Lemlist / Smartlead / Instantly** | Trial, not forever-free | Volume tools protect COGS with time-boxed Multichannel/full access | Vendor pricing pages |

**Lesson:** Forever-free works when **unit COGS is low** (credits/search) or **caps are hard** (Resend daily). Cold *sending* Free must be tiny or it burns domains + Resend bill.

### 3.2 Waitlist strategies that measure willingness to pay

From 2026 waitlist / validation playbooks ([Waitlister 2026 guide](https://waitlister.me/growth-hub/guides/saas-product-launch-waitlist), [DEV: landing + waitlist + Stripe test](https://dev.to/software_mvp-factory/validating-your-startup-idea-with-a-landing-page-waitlist-and-stripe-test-mode-in-one-weekend-257)):

1. **Email-only signup → redirect to Stripe Checkout in test mode** (real price, no charge) → measure **checkout intent %**, not vanity waitlist size.  
2. **Referral ladder:** 1 / 3 / 5 referrals → early access tiers or lifetime discount (Clubhouse-style scarcity, used carefully).  
3. **Time-boxed early bird:** Pioneer / Early / Launch price steps (10–20% off sweet spot cited in waitlist guides).  
4. **Nurture:** waitlist members convert fastest in first **30 days**; sunset inactive after 90 ([Waitlister](https://waitlister.me/growth-hub/guides/saas-product-launch-waitlist)).  
5. Stack fit for us: **Next.js + Vercel + Resend waitlist + PostHog + Stripe test** (same stack as product).

**For 성재 Phase 0:** Landing already on roadmap — wire waitlist to Resend + optional Stripe test for Indie $39.

| Play | What worked | Caveat | Source |
|------|-------------|--------|--------|
| **Repo = top of funnel** | Postiz OSS → GitHub trending launch weeks → self-hosters → paid cloud ($17k→$145k MRR narrative with MCP/agent reposition) | Needs launch concentration + community ops | [StackStarts Postiz](https://stackstarts.com/open-source-playbook-how-nevo-built-postiz-17k-mrr/), [Superframeworks Postiz](https://superframeworks.com/case-study/postiz) |
| **Open alternative to closed incumbent** | Cal.com vs Calendly — repo as trust; freemium cloud | Cal.com later moved production closed-source for security (Apr 2026); kept Cal.diy MIT fork | [Unlock SaaS Cal.com](https://unlocksaas.com/funnel-teardown/cal-com), [cal.com OSS post](https://cal.com/blog/open-source), [Cal closed-source announcement](https://cal.com/blog/cal-com-goes-closed-source-why) |
| **Self-host free / Pro managed** | Ghost(Pro) vs self-host — principle maps to structure | Ops burden on self-hosters | [Unlock SaaS Ghost](https://unlocksaas.com/funnel-teardown/ghost) |
| **Partial open (recommended for us)** | Open **Issue schema + MCP tool defs + AGENTS.md starter + Board UI template**; keep sending/warmup/billing closed | Avoids email infra liability while still trending on HN/X for agents | Product roadmap MCP Phase 2 |

**Do not open-source** the full mailer + credential vault on day 1 (abuse, ToS, deliverability liability). **Do open** the metaphor artifacts agents can install.

### 3.4 Recommended wedge sequence for this product

1. **Week −2:** Waitlist + Stripe test Intent on Indie price.  
2. **Launch:** Forever Free (hard caps) + paid Indie/Pro.  
3. **Week +2:** Public `issue-board-mcp` repo (tools + JSON schemas + example Cursor config).  
4. **Week +4:** AGENTS.md + “cold email as Issues” template repo (stars as social proof).  
5. **Later:** Optional self-host Board read-replica — only if security model is ready (learn from Cal.com’s 2026 reversal).

## 6. Concrete recommendations for 성재

1. **Price ladder:** Free (hard caps) → **Indie $39 / $29 annual** → **Pro $89 / $69** → **Agency $199 / $159** with **5 workspaces included + $25 extra**. Undercuts Smartlead’s $29 workspace tax *and* discloses it.  
2. **Meter what you are:** Gate on **active Issues + monthly touches + sending accounts + MCP**. Do **not** sell unlimited warmup / unlimited inboxes — Instantly/Smartlead own that race and COGS/abuse are ugly.  
3. **Avoid credit theater:** If AI is offered, **BYO LLM key** first; platform credits only as optional transparent pack (lemlist-style success pricing is OK; Apollo-style multi-meter confusion is not).  
4. **Agency honesty as marketing:** D6/D12 content should literally screenshot the workspace line — contrast Smartlead FAQ “$29/month per client workspace” ([smartlead.ai/pricing](https://www.smartlead.ai/pricing)).  
5. **Free = product education, not lead DB:** Cap sends near Resend free discipline (≤100/mo product-side even if Resend allows 3k) so Free users don’t torch domains or your bill ([resend.com/pricing](https://www.resend.com/pricing)).  
6. **Waitlist with Stripe test** before broad paid launch; optimize checkout intent, not email count.  
7. **Open wedge = MCP schema + AGENTS.md + Board template**, not full mailer (Cal.com’s 2026 security reversal is a warning for credential-heavy apps).  
8. **Default positioning:** Issue metaphor H1; Memory as proof; MCP as distribution channel to Cursor/agent users (Postiz-style agent reposition is the 2026 growth pattern).  
9. **COGS check monthly:** Indie 2k / Pro 10k / Agency 40k touches must stay profitable after Resend/Gmail path + Supabase; raise send packs before lowering list prices.  
10. **Re-verify prices** on Instantly / Smartlead / Lemlist / Apollo / Hunter pages the week you ship pricing UI — this doc’s numbers are 2026-09-04 snapshots.
